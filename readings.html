Readings

Close readings of poems, fiction, essays, and other creative work: a sustained response to what the writing is doing and how it is experienced by a reader.

Each reading follows the language, imagery, tone, and movement of a piece, considering how particular details contribute to the whole. It can articulate connections, tensions, and possibilities that may be difficult to see from within your own work.

Fragments and unfinished work are welcome.

•

Written Reading

A considered written response, following your work closely and reflecting on its language, form, and meaning. Questions and possibilities for further development arise from the reading itself.

For one piece or a small selection: up to 1,500 words of prose, or three short poems within five manuscript pages.

£125

Includes one brief clarification exchange. Readings of revised submissions or further discussion can be agreed separately.

Reading & Conversation

A close reading beforehand, followed by a conversation about your work and the questions you would like to explore.

For one piece or a small selection: up to 1,500 words of prose, or three short poems within five manuscript pages.

60 minutes · Online or in person (Bedford)

£200

Includes preparation and discussion, without a separate written report.
